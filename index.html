About the studio

A practice built on arriving, not achieving

We started Stillpoint after years of teaching in rooms that prized performance over presence. Here, the goal isn't the deepest backbend — it's noticing what your breath is doing while you try.

  • Small classes, never more than twelve
  • Teachers trained for 500+ hours
  • Props and modifications for every body
